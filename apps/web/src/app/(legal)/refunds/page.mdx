## 1. If you are a consumer: your 14-day right of withdrawal

If you are an individual acting outside your trade, business, craft or profession, you have a
statutory right to withdraw from your subscription **within 14 days**, without giving any reason.

The period runs for 14 days from the day the contract was concluded — that is, the day your
subscription started.

**Renewing does not start a new 14-day period.** The right applies once, to the contract you
concluded. If you later move to a different plan or a different billing interval, that is treated as a
new contract and a new 14-day period begins.

### How to withdraw

The quickest way is the **withdrawal button on the subscription page of your dashboard**. It is beside
your other billing controls. It asks you for one thing — an email address to send your confirmation
to — and then you press **Confirm withdrawal**. Nothing else is asked of you: no reason, no survey,
no phone call. We email you a confirmation of receipt straight away, and that email is your record.

You can also withdraw any other way you like, as long as it is clear and it reaches us before the 14
days are up:

- Email <SupportEmail /> — one line is sufficient; or
- Use the model form in section 5 below.

You do not have to use the form or the button, and you do not have to explain why. Sending your
message before the deadline is enough — it does not matter when we receive it.

### What you get back

**You get the full amount back.** We do not deduct anything for the time you had the service, even if
the bot published for you the whole time.

We will reimburse you without undue delay, and in any event **within 14 days** of being told you are
withdrawing. The refund is made by Paddle using the same payment method you used, at no cost to you.

The law does allow a trader to keep a proportionate amount for service already supplied — but only
where the consumer expressly asked for performance to begin during the withdrawal period, as a
separate and deliberate request. We do not ask you for that, so we do not make that deduction.

### What we do not claim

Some digital products lose the withdrawal right once the customer starts using them. **We do not
claim that.** Auto Publisher is supplied as an ongoing service rather than as a one-off download, so
starting to use it does **not** cost you your withdrawal right, and we do not ask you to waive it.

If you want to withdraw within 14 days, you can — whether or not the bot has already published
anything for you.

## 2. Cancelling a subscription

Cancelling is not the same as withdrawing.

- **Withdrawal** is the statutory right described above: it applies in the first 14 days and gets you
  a full refund.
- **Cancellation** can be done at any time. It stops the subscription renewing. The paid edition
  stays available until the end of the period you have already paid for, and that period is **not**
  refunded.

You can cancel from the subscription page in your dashboard, which takes you to Paddle's customer
portal, or by emailing <SupportEmail /> and we will arrange it.

When a paid subscription ends, the server returns to the free edition. Your channel configuration and
publishing rules are kept, but the free edition's limits apply again, so any channels above that
limit stop publishing.

If the paid bot had replaced the free bot in your server, **you will need to invite the free bot back
yourself** before publishing resumes. Discord does not allow a bot to add itself to a server, so we
cannot do this for you. The invite link is on your dashboard, and your settings are waiting for it.

## 3. Refunds outside the withdrawal period

Beyond the 14-day withdrawal period, and for business customers, refunds are not automatic — but we
are not going to hide behind that. If the service did not work as described, tell us. Where a fault
is on our side and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, you are entitled to a price reduction
or to end the contract, and we will arrange the appropriate refund through Paddle.

Consumers additionally have the conformity rights described in the [Terms of Service](/terms),
section 4. Those rights are free of charge and are not limited by this page.

## 4. If you are a business

The right of withdrawal is a consumer right and does not apply to business customers. You can cancel
at any time as described in section 2. Refunds outside that are at our discretion, except where the
service failed to conform to what we agreed.
